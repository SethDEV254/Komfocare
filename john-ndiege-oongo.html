A Life Well Lived

“A devoted family man, a faithful servant of God, a hardworking and respected member of the community.”

Life History

Birth
The late John Ndiege Oongo was born on 1st July 1954 to the late Andrea Oongo and the late Mama Damaris Oyugi at the late village in Karungu Division. He was a brother to the late Ezekiel Miguna, Kilopia Nyangwecha, the late Rosa Ochola, the late Zablon Owigo, Pali Ongo, Aoko, Richard Tambo, the late Wilson Oyoro, John Omwanda, the late Godorn Odero, Simon Sime, and Mike Oongo.

Education
Mzee Ndiege started his early education at Rabwrao Primary School and later moved to Sori Primary School where he sat his CPE. After having done his final exams, he joined JohnBosco Secondary School in Nairobi where he did his exams.

Marriage
The late John Ndiege Oongo got married to two wives namely Jenifer (1st Wife) and Julita (2nd Wife). He was blessed with seven children (3 sons and 4 daughters), namely Omori Calvin, Juli Auma, the late Quinter Adhiambo (Ogego), Sharon Akinyi, the late Austin, Fidel and Slovakia.

Work
After his education, John Ndiege worked with FMCO Glass Company in Nairobi. Later on he worked with other companies such as Kenya Brewery Company in Kisumu, Ndeha Construction Company, Kenya Cold Storage Company and lastly worked with Al-Na-Quel Company up to the time of his demise.

Christian and Social Life
He was a strong and active member of Bongu SDA Church and maintained his faith until his demise. He was known to be a respectful, hardworking and loving member of the community.

Eulogy

Mzee John Ndiege Oongo will be remembered as a devoted family man, a faithful servant of God, a hardworking and respected member of the community. His love, guidance and generosity touched many lives and his legacy will continue to live on in the hearts of his family, friends and all who knew him.
