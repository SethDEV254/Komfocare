A LIFE WELL LIVED

Life History

Birth: The late John Ndiege Oongo was born on 1st July 1954 to the late Andrea Oongo and the late Mama Damaris Oyugi at the late village in Karungu Division.

Education: Mzee Ndiege started his early education at Rabwrao Primary School and later moved to Sori Primary School where he sat his CPE. He later joined JohnBosco Secondary School in Nairobi.

Marriage: He was married to Jenifer (1st Wife) and Julita (2nd Wife), and was blessed with seven children.

Work: He worked with FMCO Glass Company in Nairobi and later with Kenya Brewery Company, Ndeha Construction Company, Kenya Cold Storage Company and Al-Na-Quel Company.

Christian and Social Life: He was a strong and active member of Bongu SDA Church and maintained his faith until his demise. He was known as a respectful, hardworking and loving member of the community.
