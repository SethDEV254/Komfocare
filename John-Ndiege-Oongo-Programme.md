# John Ndiege Oongo

## In Loving Memory

**1 July 1954 — 20 August 2026**

> A life well lived. A devoted family man, a faithful servant of God, a hardworking and respected member of the community.

---

## Life History

### Birth
The late John Ndiege Oongo was born on 1st July 1954 to the late Andrea Oongo and the late Mama Damaris Oyugi at the late village in Karungu Division. He was a brother to the late Ezekiel Miguna, Kilopia Nyangwecha, the late Rosa Ochola, the late Zablon Owigo, Pali Oongo, Aoko, Richard Tambo, the late Wilson Oyoro, John Omwanda, the late Godorn Odero, Simon Sime, and Mike Oongo.

### Education
Mzee Ndiege started his early education at Rabwrao Primary School and later moved to Sori Primary School where he sat his CPE. After having done his final exams, he joined JohnBosco Secondary School in Nairobi where he did his exams.

### Marriage and Family
The late John Ndiege Oongo was married to Jenifer (1st Wife) and Julita (2nd Wife), and was blessed with seven children.

### Work
After his education, John Ndiege worked with FMCO Glass Company in Nairobi and later with other companies including Kenya Brewery Company in Kisumu, Ndeha Construction Company, Kenya Cold Storage Company and Al-Na-Quel Company.

### Christian and Social Life
He was a strong and active member of Bongu SDA Church and maintained his faith until his demise. He was known to be respectful, hardworking and loving member of the community.
